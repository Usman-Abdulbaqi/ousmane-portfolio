About

Good e-commerce design sits where visual design, usability, and conversion meet.

I'm an e-commerce website designer & developer specializing in Shopify, WooCommerce, and WordPress, with a strong focus on store design, development, UI/UX, responsive experiences, and website redesigns.

I work with businesses, entrepreneurs, and growing brands that need more than just a website that looks good — the goal is an online store that communicates the brand clearly, provides a smooth customer experience, and supports business growth.

Whether you're launching a new e-commerce store, redesigning an outdated website, migrating to Shopify, improving an existing WooCommerce store, or simply looking for a better user experience, I can help turn the idea into a polished digital storefront.

  • Shopify store design & development
  • Shopify 2.0 store customization
  • E-commerce website design & redesign
  • WooCommerce & WordPress development
  • UI/UX & responsive web design
  • Product, collection & category pages
  • Landing page design
  • Conversion-focused optimization
